I'm **Sooraj Sathyanarayanan**, a **Program Manager at Brave** and a cybersecurity and privacy researcher. I hold an **MS in Cybersecurity from Northeastern University**.

I'm interested in how software handles people's data, how security claims hold up under examination, and how to make the findings useful to the people building and using those systems.

## Work and research

I joined Brave in December 2025. My background spans security and privacy assessment, penetration testing, digital forensics, infrastructure hardening, and open-source work.

My coauthored publications cover **security in edge computing** and **context-aware blockchain validation**. The [Research page]({{< relref "/research/_index.md" >}}) lists the papers and their original publisher records. My work is also indexed on [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=NwIwfYkAAAAJ).

My research on the privacy of fitness wearables received grant support from the [Ludlow Institute](https://www.ludlowinstitute.org/grants) through Timechain Consulting, beginning in 2025.

I also develop practical security resources and work on [Site Behavior Lab](https://github.com/iAnonymous3000/site-behavior-lab), an open-source project for examining website behavior. Its reports describe observations from automated visits, with the conditions and limitations needed to interpret them.
