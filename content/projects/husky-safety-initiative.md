## Project overview

I created the Husky Safety Initiative as a collection of privacy and security resources for students, faculty, and staff in the Northeastern community.

The list brings together resources for checking data breaches, managing passwords, enabling multi-factor authentication, and choosing communication and storage tools. It is intended to help readers explore options for their own needs.
