## Security, privacy and practical software

I'm Sooraj Sathyanarayanan, a Program Manager at Brave and a cybersecurity and privacy researcher. I build tools, investigate software behavior and explain security findings with their evidence and limitations.

### Software you can inspect

At Brave, I develop [Nightly change-review tooling]({{< relref "/projects/_index.md" >}}#brave-nightly-change-tracker). [Site Behavior Lab]({{< relref "/projects/_index.md" >}}#site-behavior-lab) records website behavior during automated browser visits. My [selected projects]({{< relref "/projects/_index.md" >}}) also include browser-based tools for investigating spyware indicators, inspecting metadata and examining QR codes.
