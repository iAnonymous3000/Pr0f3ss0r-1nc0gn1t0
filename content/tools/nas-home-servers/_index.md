A NAS (network-attached storage) keeps shared files, photo libraries, and device backups on storage you control. Choose by the services you need, how you will maintain the device, and where you will keep a separate backup.

Local storage does not establish zero data collection or automatic encryption. Review vendor accounts, remote access, analytics, update settings, and encryption keys separately. RAID and snapshots on the same device do not replace an off-device backup.

Synology DiskStation supports a broader range of photo, sync, backup, and application services on compatible models. Ubiquiti UNAS focuses on file storage and backups through UniFi Drive. Both use proprietary vendor software.

Camera systems have their own category: [Home Security Cameras]({{< relref "/tools/home-security-cameras" >}}). For files that need encryption before reaching the NAS, see [File Encryption]({{< relref "/tools/file-encryption" >}}).
