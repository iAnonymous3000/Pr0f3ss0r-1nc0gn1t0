Bitrefill sells digital gift cards, mobile top-ups, and eSIMs with cryptocurrency payment options, including Bitcoin and Lightning. It provides a way to spend cryptocurrency on products from participating merchants, subject to regional availability and issuer rules.

Bitrefill's terms allow guest gift-card and mobile-top-up purchases and require a working email address or phone number. Guest checkout alone does not guarantee anonymity.

Privacy and practical limits:

- Bitrefill's privacy policy describes internal guest profiles that group orders from the same browser session. These may link checkout email, IP address and location, browser and device data, fraud-prevention fingerprints, recipient details, payment information, and purchase history. The policy also identifies analytics, fraud-prevention, and transaction-monitoring partners.
- Identity verification can be requested for purchases, continued use, or some refunds. Requirements depend on the product, customer, and compliance checks; this is not a universal no-KYC service.
- Redeeming a gift card can require a merchant account or other identifying information. For example, Bitrefill's Amazon US instructions call for an account with another payment method and a verified US address. Check the specific product's country and redemption rules before buying.
- Some products cost more than face value, and service or payment-related fees can apply. Review the final price, conversion rate, and applicable network fees before paying.
- A valid gift card whose code has been revealed generally cannot be refunded. Unrevealed cards may qualify at Bitrefill's discretion; failed or invalid products have separate refund provisions. Bitrefill's terms set a 30-day request window for eligible refunds. Incorrect mobile top-ups generally cannot be cancelled.

**Review scope (October 25, 2026):** Public product, policy, and support documentation. I have not tested checkout, gift-card redemption, or network behavior. Data-collection statements here describe published disclosures.
