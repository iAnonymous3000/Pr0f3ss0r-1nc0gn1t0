Proton Mail provides automatic end-to-end encryption for message bodies and attachments exchanged between Proton Mail users. Key features include:
- Automatic end-to-end encryption between Proton Mail users
- Password-protected emails or configured PGP for end-to-end encrypted communication with external recipients
- Zero-access encryption for stored message bodies and attachments
- Based in Switzerland
- Free tier available
- Available across all major platforms

Ordinary email exchanged with external providers such as Gmail is not end-to-end encrypted unless you use password-protected emails or PGP. TLS protects messages in transit when supported, but the external provider can still access them. Subject lines and sender and recipient addresses are not end-to-end encrypted.

See [what Proton Mail encrypts](https://proton.me/support/what-is-encrypted-within-protonmail) and [how to use PGP with external contacts](https://proton.me/support/how-to-use-pgp).
