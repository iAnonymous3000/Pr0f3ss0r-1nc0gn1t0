California's Delete Request and Opt-out Platform (DROP) lets eligible residents submit one free, ongoing deletion request to registered data brokers. [Official overview](https://privacy.ca.gov/drop/).

- Verify California residency before submitting.
- The deletion request requires your name, date of birth and ZIP code. Additional identifiers are optional and may improve matching.
- Broker processing began August 1, 2026. Brokers must check DROP at least every 45 days; initial status reporting can take up to 90 days.
- After initial processing, brokers must re-check and delete new matching, non-exempt information at least every 45 days.
- Save your eight-digit DROP ID privately. Do not share it; it gives access to request status.

Deletion depends on matching and legal exemptions. DROP does not require deletion of information you gave a business directly (first-party data), exempt records or publicly available information. Submitting a request does not confirm that every broker found or deleted your data. Review each broker's reported status and update your profile if your information changes.

See [How DROP works](https://privacy.ca.gov/drop/how-drop-works/) for eligibility, matching, status definitions and exclusions.
