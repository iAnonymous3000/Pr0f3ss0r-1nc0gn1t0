**Keep your current router if it still receives security updates and meets your needs.** Consider replacing it when support, coverage, speed, or control falls short.

Prioritize three things:

- **Maintenance you can manage:** a clear update process and a way to recover from problems.
- **Local control:** administration from your own network, with clear information about cloud features and data collection.
- **A complete setup:** enough Wi-Fi coverage and wired ports, with all required hardware included in your budget.

The choices below serve different needs: **UniFi for convenience, OpenWrt One for hands-on control, and DEC750 for advanced networks.** Open-source software alone does not determine the recommendation.

Use **gigabit Internet** as a starting point. Faster routing, a whole-network VPN, intrusion prevention, and traffic shaping need separate performance evidence. The notes distinguish documented specifications from measured results and include purchasing, support, and manufacturing details.
