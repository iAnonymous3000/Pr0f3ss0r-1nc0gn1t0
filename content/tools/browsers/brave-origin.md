Brave Origin is a streamlined version of Brave for people who want its built-in ad and tracker blocking with fewer extra features. It retains Brave Shields and receives the same Chromium patches and security updates as other Brave releases.

There are two ways to use it:

- **Standalone desktop app:** extra features such as Leo, News, Rewards, VPN, Wallet, and Tor windows are compiled out of the build.
- **Upgrade to the existing Brave app:** an Origin settings panel lets you manage those features, which are disabled by default. The upgrade is available on Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, and iOS; mobile does not have a separate standalone Origin app.

Brave documents removal of the daily usage ping, crash logs, and privacy-preserving product analytics (P3A) in Origin. This does not mean that the browser makes no network requests: updates and enabled services still need connections.

Origin is available through a one-time US$59.99 purchase covering both options across your devices, subject to activation rate limits. It is free on Linux. Regular Brave remains free and supported, and many unwanted features can also be hidden or disabled there without buying Origin.

Origin is a fit for a simpler Brave setup. It does not provide anonymity, and the standalone app excludes Brave's Tor-window feature.
