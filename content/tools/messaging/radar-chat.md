Radar Chat combines messaging on Signal's network with a Bitcoin Lightning wallet. It is an independent project from the team behind Cake Wallet and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Signal Messenger. Its [official documentation](https://radar.chat/support) describes:

- End-to-end encrypted messages and calls using the Signal protocol
- Compatibility with existing Signal contacts
- Self-custodial Bitcoin Lightning payments inside conversations, with wallet keys held on the device
- Free iOS and Android apps, with no subscription tiers
- AGPLv3 source code for [iOS](https://github.com/radar-labs/Radar) and [Android](https://github.com/radar-labs/radar-android)

The [privacy policy](https://radar.chat/privacy) says messaging uses Signal's servers, optional contact discovery processes address-book information, and payment nodes receive your IP address and transaction or channel data. Radar says its own nodes do not retain that data; third-party nodes have their own practices. The developer's [Google Play disclosure](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.radarlabs.radar) also lists personal information and contacts as data it may collect, and contacts as data it may share.

Keep a secure offline wallet backup: Radar says it cannot recover a lost seed phrase or funds. There are currently no built-in fiat purchases, cash-outs, or cryptocurrency swaps. These are documented provider claims, rather than an independent security assessment of Radar.
