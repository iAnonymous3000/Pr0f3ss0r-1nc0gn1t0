## Publications

### Adaptive Fuzzy Logic Framework for Context-Aware Blockchain Security

**Sooraj Sathyanarayanan and Varshitha Manjunath.**

In Sameena Pathan, Shiva Raj Pokhrel, Gang Li, and Catarina Barata (eds.), *Computational Intelligence and Soft Computing*, CISCom 2025. Communications in Computer and Information Science, volume 2853, pp. 191–199. Springer, Singapore, 2026. First online March 24, 2026.

### Quantum-Resilient Zero-Trust Architectures in Edge Computing Networks: Theoretical Foundations and Complexity Bounds

**Varshitha Manjunath, Sooraj Sathyanarayanan, Rajesh Purushothaman, Arun Kumar Elengovan, Jeevan Shanbhag, and Rakesh Keshava.**

Theoretical research into quantum-resilient zero-trust architectures for edge networks, including security assumptions, communication complexity, and privacy tradeoffs.

[Publisher record](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/11430991) · [DOI: 10.1109/ICCA66035.2025.11430991](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICCA66035.2025.11430991)

2025 IEEE 11th International Conference on Edge Computing and Scalable Cloud (EdgeCom). Conference held November 7–9, 2025; added to IEEE Xplore January 22, 2026.
